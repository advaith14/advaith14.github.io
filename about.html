About Me

I'm a Data Engineer with hands-on experience building end-to-end data pipelines across cloud and on-premises environments. My work spans ingestion from JDBC databases, REST APIs, and flat files through to transformation, testing, and delivery into analytical targets like Snowflake, Databricks Unity Catalog, and Azure Blob Storage.

I enjoy making complex data systems reliable and maintainable — whether that means building a reusable PySpark transformation library, designing Delta Lake merge strategies, or writing regression tests that catch data drift before it reaches production.

Outside of work I'm learning more about data architecture patterns, Snowflake Cortex ML, and modern data stack tooling like dbt.
